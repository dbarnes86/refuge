The idea

You built the company. You raised the round, ran the team, made the flight. The last ten years were spent at full speed, and the calendar shows it.

Refuge is the counterweight. A working estate in Saint-Émilion where a small group of families keep a permanent foothold — a place that is already yours when you arrive, where the days are long, the phones are quiet, and the people around the table chose the same thing you did.

Not a hotel. Not a resort. An estate, held in common by people who decided the next twenty years should look different.
